How it works

Tap to collect

Every sticker has an NFC chip. Hold your phone against it and the rat is yours.

A card for every rat

Each rat has a name, a drawing and a description. Flip the card to read its story.

A map with hints

Rats you have not found show up as a search circle, not an exact pin. The hunting is up to you.

A journal of your finds

The app remembers when and in which city you found each rat.

No account, no ads

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy. Your collection lives on your phone.

Sticker gone?

Stickers get damaged or removed. Report a missing one and its status gets updated in the app.
